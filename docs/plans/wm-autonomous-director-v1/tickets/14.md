# 14 - Apply lasting quest consequences with offline catch-up

## Parent

[WM Autonomous Director V1](https://github.com/Dicetar/wow-wm-project/issues/5)

## What to build

A justified world change can retire or fail an accepted quest and replace its progression coherently, including for a player who was offline when the event occurred.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Extend the proven continuity operation family to a durable event outcome with explicit old-to-new quest/progress mappings.
- [ ] Implement and contract missing fail/retire transition verbs narrowly if required; do not weaken existing managed-quest guards globally.
- [ ] Stage the replacement and verify its feasibility before committing a lasting transition; use evidence from the world-impact preview.
- [ ] Record affected online and offline players and reconcile on return, preserving earned progress where semantically appropriate and preventing repeated reward claims.
- [ ] Explain the consequence and continuation in-game; do not leave old objectives active and impossible or silently delete rewarded history.
- [ ] Persist transition progress across crashes and detect intervening player progression or world edits before resuming.
- [ ] Keep a defined conditional recovery/compensation path for technical failure without pretending every intentional narrative consequence must be undone.
- [ ] Test partial completion, completed-but-unrewarded state, rewarded state, two participants, offline return, restart, and conflicting edits; capture representative in-client proof.

## Blocked by

- [Run and restore a temporary world event without stranding quests](https://github.com/Dicetar/wow-wm-project/issues/18)

## Verification and scope

Repo/native transition tests plus live online/offline quest-continuity proof. This does not promise automatic support for all stock quest scripts.

Use the existing WM workflow and relevant native/content skills. Preserve the Python/native ownership split, typed gates, current marker targeting, and unrelated working-tree changes. This is a complete user-visible or publicly verifiable slice; tests should exercise outcomes rather than source layout.
