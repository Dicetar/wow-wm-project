# World Master

WM observes play, interprets player intent, and creates context-sensitive experiences for the selected character. This glossary records agreed product language, not implementation status.

## Language

**Experience beat**:
A meaningful moment in a character's journey, such as a discovery, encounter, choice, reward, or consequence. Its identity is the change experienced by the player, not the quest, message, item, or other delivery surface used to realize it.

**Grounded opportunity**:
A possible experience beat supported by observed play, relevant character history, or explicit player direction. It is a possibility to evaluate, not an obligation to interrupt the player.

**Player obligation**:
An accepted or already-started experience, earned outcome, or promised consequence that WM must resolve coherently. Changing initiative or pausing new stories does not erase these obligations.

**Policy-bounded autonomy**:
WM's authority to choose reactions and create or grant supported content without per-action confirmation, within operator-approved capabilities and limits. Content must still pass the required validation and release gates; work requiring new native mechanics, client patches, or server restarts is staged for review.

**Supported content**:
Content that WM can realize through its available, validated capabilities, with the required player-visible presentation and deployment requirements satisfied. A generated description alone does not make content supported.

**Development request**:
A task describing a missing capability that prevents WM from fulfilling an intended experience, for the operator to hand to a coding agent. Creating the task does not make the capability available; implementation and verification must precede its use in play.

**World continuity**:
The requirement that WM's changes to existing world content have a meaningful cause and account for affected gameplay through a replacement, transition, or defined temporary interruption and restoration. Shared-world changes, including removing quest-related spawns, are within WM's intended authority; an in-world explanation alone does not replace handling the affected content.

**Quest transition**:
A deliberate change to a player's quest path caused by events in the world, which may include failing or retiring an accepted quest. WM explains the consequence, provides a coherent continuation, and preserves earned progress where appropriate; a stranded quest with no handled outcome is a defect, not a transition.

**Initiative preset**:
The selected level of unsolicited WM activity: On Demand, Moderate, or Active, with Moderate as the default. It governs pacing rather than permission to bypass validation or world continuity.

**On Demand**:
An initiative preset in which WM observes and remembers but begins new experiences only when requested. Already-started stories, accepted quests, promised consequences, active events, and required cleanup continue automatically.

**Author's Notes**:
Persistent, user-authored direction for WM, kept in a distinct, user-accessible place apart from memories of events and WM's own inferences. Standing requests such as "give me fewer quests" remain in effect across sessions unless changed or explicitly temporary.

**Note scope**:
The intended reach of an Author's Note: World for shared-world direction or Character for direction concerning one character. In-game instructions default to the speaking character; the panel offers explicit World and Character scopes.

**Note firmness**:
An optional advanced distinction between a preference and a firm constraint, with ordinary Author's Notes treated as preferences by default. More specific preferences may take precedence over broader preferences, but preferences do not override firm constraints; conflicting applicable firm constraints require clarification.
